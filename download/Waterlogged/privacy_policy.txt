Privacy Policy for Waterlogged
Last updated: October 18, 2026

Introduction
hrb116 ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Waterlogged Wear OS and Android companion application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we access, collect, use, store, and protect information—including Health Data—in relation to our App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Health Data and Health Connect Integration
Waterlogged integrates with Android Health Connect to provide water intake logging and hydration goal tracking features.

- Data Types Accessed:
  * Hydration (Read): We access your daily hydration records to calculate and display your total daily water intake and your progress toward your target hydration goal.
  * Hydration (Write): When you log water intake on your Wear OS watch or companion app, the App writes the recorded water volume and timestamp directly to Health Connect.

- Purpose of Data Use:
  * To log your water intake events.
  * To aggregate your daily water intake and visually display your daily progress bar and goal on your watch tile and companion app.
  * Health data is processed locally on your device strictly to provide this core hydration tracking functionality.

2. Health Data Sharing and Restrictions
In accordance with Google Play's Health Apps Policy and Health Connect Developer Policies:
- No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal or health data to any third parties.
- No Advertising or Marketing: Health Data accessed through Health Connect is never used, transferred, or disclosed for third-party advertising, marketing, user profiling, or retargeting.
- No Unauthorized External Transfers: Your Health Data stays on your device and within Android Health Connect. We do not transmit or store your Health Data on external servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics services.
- No Lending or Credit Decisions: Health data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

3. Preferences & Local Storage
The App stores your chosen water preset preferences (e.g. glass and bottle sizes, daily hydration goal) locally on your device and syncs them to your paired Wear OS watch. We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location, or device identifiers.

4. User Control and Data Deletion
- Permissions Control: You have complete control over Health Connect permissions. You can grant, deny, or revoke access to Read or Write Hydration data at any time via Android Settings -> Security & Privacy -> Privacy -> Health Connect -> Waterlogged.
- Data Deletion: Because health data is managed by Android Health Connect, you can view, edit, or delete any of your hydration records directly from the Health Connect app on your device.

5. Security
We take the security of your data seriously. All interactions with Health Connect use Android's secure, sandboxed on-device APIs.

6. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal or health information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
